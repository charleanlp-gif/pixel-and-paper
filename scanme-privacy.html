ScanMe Privacy Policy

Published by Pixel & Paper

Effective date: 4 November 2026

1. Introduction

Pixel & Paper respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how ScanMe handles information when you use the application.

2. Camera Permission

ScanMe requests camera permission to scan barcodes and QR codes.

Camera access is used for scanning. The application does not provide a feature for saving or uploading camera photographs. Camera permission can be managed through your device settings.

3. Information Stored

ScanMe stores scanning records in a local SQLite database on your device.

These records include the scanned code value, code type, scan date, scan time and timestamp. The application also assigns each record a database identifier.

Scan history is stored locally rather than being uploaded to a Pixel & Paper scan-history server.

4. Exporting and Sharing Data

ScanMe allows you to export selected scan information into a CSV file.

CSV files are generated on your device. When exporting, ScanMe opens your device's sharing interface so you can choose where to send or save the file.

If you share exported information with another application or service, that service's own privacy practices may apply.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Scan records remain in the application's local database until you clear them or the application data is otherwise removed.

You can delete saved scan history by opening Settings and selecting Clear Scan History.

Clearing scan history does not delete CSV files that you previously exported or shared. Those files must be managed separately.

6. Advertising and Analytics

ScanMe does not include built-in advertising or user-behaviour analytics features.

7. Data Security

ScanMe stores its scanning records in the application's local database. Users should protect their devices and exercise care when sharing exported files.

No electronic storage method can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes to This Policy

Pixel & Paper may update this Privacy Policy when ScanMe's functionality or data-handling practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
